Background

Why these songs exist

In March 2020 my wife suffered a brain haemorrhage. She has been in a persistent vegetative state ever since.

She cannot speak. But she answers — I see it in her eyes and in her face. I tell her everything, and she lets me know what she makes of it. Including this website.

I wanted to write one song for her. It turned into fifty.

Out of that came the album Erwacht and later a book to go with it. It is about someone who, after a rupture, no longer knows whether he is still alive — and who finds his way back. I did not have to invent any of it.

If you are going through the same

Then you know how lonely it is. How few people understand that someone can be present and missing at the same time. How hard it is to carry on a life that was meant for two.

I cannot tell you that it gets easier. It does not get easier. But it becomes bearable. You find a way to carry on without forgetting — and sometimes something comes of it that you never expected.

For me it was songs.

Where you are not alone

I belong to a support group of the SelbstHilfeVerband – FORUM GEHIRN e. V., the German national association for people with acquired brain injury and their families. The people there carry the same questions you do — and answers you will not find in any leaflet.

There are groups across Germany. If you do not know where to start: start there.
