Real songs. Real feelings.

Music connects.

I live in Marburg, Germany, and play guitar and keyboard. Music has been with me all my life; I have been writing my own songs since 2023. My lyrics work through personal experience and loss — but hope as well.

I record my parts myself. Not every note lands; AI helps me clean things up afterwards. The words are mine.
