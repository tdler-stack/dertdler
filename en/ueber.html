About

Thorsten Diehl

I was born in 1971 and live in Marburg, Germany. Music has been with me all my life — I play guitar and keyboard, and in 2023 I began writing my own songs.

My lyrics deal with what genuinely moves me: personal experience, loss, hope and the search for meaning.

I studied computer science and education, and spent a good deal of time with philosophy. In 1999 I was diagnosed with multiple sclerosis — since then I have lived with impaired sensation in my hands. Four years ago I began eating according to Ayurvedic principles, to treat my body more carefully.

2020 changed everything: my wife suffered a brain haemorrhage and has been in a persistent vegetative state ever since. That experience changed my life — and it brought my music. Music is expression, release, and an attempt to reach someone with words where words really fail.
