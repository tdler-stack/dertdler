Erwacht — a book following the album

What lies between the notes has no room on an album. So I wrote it down.

Seventeen chapters that follow the emotional path of the album and yet stand on their own. The story of someone who, after a final loss, no longer knows whether he is truly alive — and who finds his way back.

“Erwacht is not a book about forgetting. It is a book about carrying on. About the love that does not end just because someone is no longer standing beside us.”

Paperback and e-book in preparation

The album tells the same story in songs. And here is why they exist.
