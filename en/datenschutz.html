In short

This site sets no cookies and evaluates nothing. There are no analytics tools, no advertising networks and no social media buttons reading along. Fonts and images are served from the same server as the site itself.

The only exception is the Spotify player on the music page — and it loads only when you explicitly load it. See below.

Access data

When you open the site, the provider's web server stores technically necessary data in log files: the address requested, the time, the volume of data transferred, browser type and operating system, and the IP address. This data serves secure operation only and is not combined with other sources. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in trouble-free operation. The logs are deleted after a short period.

Hosting

This site is hosted by 1blu AG, Riedemannweg 60, 13627 Berlin, Germany. A data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR is in place with the provider.

Getting in touch

If you write to me by email or call me, I use your details solely to answer your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) or (f) GDPR. The details are deleted as soon as they are no longer needed and no statutory retention obligation applies.

Spotify player on the music page

On the Music page you can display a player from Spotify. It does not load by itself. At first you see only a notice with a button. Only when you click it does your browser connect to servers of Spotify AB, Regeringsgatan 19, 111 53 Stockholm, Sweden.

In doing so, your IP address and technical details about your device are transmitted to Spotify. If you are logged in to Spotify, the request can be linked to your account. The basis for this: your consent under Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR, which you give by clicking and which you can withdraw at any time by reloading the page and not opening the player again.

I have no influence on the processing carried out by Spotify. Details can be found in Spotify's own privacy policy.

Links to other providers

This site links to third party services such as streaming platforms. Only when you click such a link does the provider concerned learn anything about you. Their own privacy policies apply to their data processing; I have no influence on it.

Your rights

  • access to the data stored about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR) and restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • objection to processing (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • complaint to a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The competent authority is the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

An email to the address given above is enough for any of these.
