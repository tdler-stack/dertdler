Privacy for the “TDler Spiele” app

This policy applies to the app TDler Spiele (package name de.tdler.spiele). For the website, see the privacy policy of the website.

In short

The app collects no personal data and sends nothing to me or to anyone else. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics tools, no crash reports and no advertising identifier. The app works entirely without an internet connection.

What is stored on your device

So that your settings and unfinished games are kept, the app stores the following locally on your device:

  • the language you chose
  • the design you chose
  • the player name you enter yourself — optional, the field may stay empty
  • the most recent game state for chess, backgammon, Skat and Doppelkopf

This data sits in the protected area that the operating system assigns to each app. Other apps cannot reach it. It is never transmitted. If you uninstall the app, the operating system deletes it along with the app.

If you enter your real name as the player name, that too stays on your device alone. You can change or delete it at any time in the app's settings.

Permissions

The app requests exactly one permission: INTERNET. It is required on technical grounds by the development framework used (React Native). The app itself makes no network connections. It requests no access to photos, files, contacts, location, camera, microphone or any other part of your device.

No sharing

No data is passed to third parties, because none is collected. No third party components for advertising, statistics or error analysis are built in.

App stores

The app is distributed through Google Play and Apple's App Store. In doing so, Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and Apple Distribution International Ltd., Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Hollyhill, Cork, Ireland, process data under their own responsibility — regarding installation, account and payment, for example. I have no influence on this. What applies are the privacy notices of the respective provider. As the developer I receive only aggregated, anonymous statistics, such as the number of installations. Individual users cannot be identified in them.

Children

The app is intended for all age groups and contains no content aimed specifically at children. Since no data is collected, no personal records arise even when children use it.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to complain to a supervisory authority. The competent authority is the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

In practice a request for access comes to nothing, because I hold no data about you. You delete the data stored on your device yourself, by uninstalling the app or resetting its data in your device settings.

Changes

Should a future version of the app process data — in the case of in-app purchases, for example — this policy will be updated beforehand.
